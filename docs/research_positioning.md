# Research Positioning Note

Ontology RAG Guardrail should be positioned as ontology-grounded trivalent validation for RAG and agent systems.

The defensible research claim is narrow:

> Given configured evidence, ontology facts, policy facts, and compliance rules, the runtime can produce auditable trivalent decisions that distinguish support, contradiction, and insufficient evidence.

## Scientific Lineage

GroundCite contributes claim/span-level groundedness discipline, abstention risk framing, evaluation metrics, and reproducibility habits. Ontology RAG Guardrail adapts that discipline for runtime enforcement rather than presenting it as a complete scientific benchmark by itself.

Legacy modules contribute reusable ideas around trivalent truth mapping, ontology graph representation, symbolic inference, validation cascades, and proof ledger patterns. The old AGI framing is explicitly out of scope.

## What The Product Does Not Prove

The product does not prove:

- real-world truth.
- legal compliance in the absence of legal review.
- elimination of hallucinations.
- completeness of a retrieval corpus.
- scientific superiority without a controlled benchmark.
- safe autonomous action without system-level controls.

## Research Questions

Useful research questions include:

- Does trivalent abstention reduce unsupported answer exposure compared with binary allow/block guardrails?
- Does ontology versioning improve auditability after policy or knowledge changes?
- Does explicit action authorization reduce unsafe or unauthorized tool calls in agent workflows?
- Can claim-level proof records make RAG failure analysis more reproducible?

## Evidence Standard

Scientific claims should be backed by frozen datasets, fixed evaluation protocols, versioned code, and a claims ledger that separates supported results from hypotheses and product aspirations.
